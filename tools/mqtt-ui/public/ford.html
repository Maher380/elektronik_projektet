DRIVE STYLE

Stop the car to change it

ManualByRemote is what the car boots on. GapCalibration spins the measured wheel under power for about a minute: lift the car before starting it. SpeedCalibration drives the car on the floor, 5 m forward and 5 m back, twelve times: give it a straight, clear run of 5 m plus about 0.5 m at each end.
